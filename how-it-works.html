How it works

From first call to weekly reports.

A clear, five-step process so you always know what's being handled, how, and with what results.

01

Tell Us What You Need

We discuss your business, customers, communication channels and the specific support you need.

02

We Agree on the Scope

We define services, working hours, days, communication channels, KPIs, reporting, payment and start date.

03

You Provide Access & Resources

Depending on the project, you provide approved access and business information such as WhatsApp Business, CRM, email, scripts, product information and customer or order details.

04

We Work Remotely

Girlley handles the agreed responsibilities remotely as an independent service provider, in your brand voice.

05

You Receive Updates

You receive agreed reports showing activity, outcomes, customer feedback and other relevant KPIs.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Our services are designed for growing businesses that need professional customer support without necessarily hiring a full in-house team.

Do you handle sales?

Yes. Depending on the agreed service, we can assist with enquiries, lead follow-up, outbound calls, appointment setting, order assistance and other sales-support activities.

Can you work with WhatsApp only?

Yes. We can work with WhatsApp, calls, email and other agreed channels.

Do you provide your own CRM?

We can work with your existing CRM or agreed tools. For smaller businesses, we can set up a simple workflow using tools like Google Sheets, WhatsApp Business and other appropriate systems.

Can I hire you just for calls?

Yes. We can structure a service around outbound calls, lead follow-up, appointment setting or telemarketing.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Girlley is a strictly remote service provider.

Are you an employee of my company?

No. Girlley provides services as an independent contractor/service provider under an agreed scope of work.
